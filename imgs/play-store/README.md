# Google Play listing screenshots

Phone screenshots for the Running and Cycling app listings are grouped by flavor and Play Store locale:

- `running/en-US/` and `cycling/en-US/`: English (United States)
- `running/es-419/` and `cycling/es-419/`: Spanish (Latin America)

Each locale has five PNGs in listing order: ready, running, splits, paused, and history. All images are 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16). The Portuguese (Brazil) Play Store listing was left unchanged and is not included in this set.
